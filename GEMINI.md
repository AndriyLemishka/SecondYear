# Antigravity & Gemini Project Rules

## Always Active: Humanize Writing Protocol
Whenever writing or editing reports, lab descriptions, documentation, comments, or explanations:
1. Follow the workspace skill at [`.agents/skills/humanize-writing/SKILL.md`](.agents/skills/humanize-writing/SKILL.md).
2. Maintain high burstiness (vary sentence lengths between 25+ words and 1-4 words).
3. Strictly avoid LLM clichés:
   - EN: delve, tapestry, beacon, testament, pivotal, paramount, meticulous, navigating, crucial role.
   - UK: «відіграє ключову роль», «у сучасному швидкоплинному світі», «важливо зазначити», «гармонійне поєднання».
4. Never generate boilerplate intro/outro sandwich structures. Jump straight into the core substance.
